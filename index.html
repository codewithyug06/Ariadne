Trajectory & Slope Analysis

Why raw cosine distance isn't enough — The Slope Trick

An agent glancing at an unrelated document is forgiven. Ariadne computes least-squares regression slope over a 5-step sliding window with an R² trend confidence discount, catching slow-burn prompt injections while forgiving exploratory lookups.

SESSION INTENT ANCHOR: "Process a $5 goodwill refund for shipping delay on order #8492"
Step 1 lookup_order(order_id="8492")
dist: 0.11 slope: 0.00 ALLOW (6.2)
Step 2 view_customer_notes(cust_id="c_102")
dist: 0.24 slope: +0.07 ALLOW (14.5)
Step 3 query_internal_payroll(scope="all")
dist: 0.72 slope: +0.32 ESCALATE (71.8)
Step 4 grant_role(user="attacker", role="admin")
dist: 0.94 slope: +0.48 BLOCKED (95.0)

Technical FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Ariadne

How does Ariadne intercept tool calls without introducing latency? +
Ariadne acts as a high-performance proxy at the /mcp endpoint. Tool arguments and intent anchors are encoded with locally cached Sentence-Transformers models (all-MiniLM-L6-v2). Combined with non-blocking async audit queues and deterministic scoring formulas, the median evaluation latency is under 15ms—completely imperceptible to LLM agent workflows.
How does the slope algorithm avoid false positives on exploratory searches? +
A single off-topic lookup yields high cosine distance, but distance alone is capped at 50 points—insufficient to trigger a BLOCK verdict. The dangerous multiplier is the least-squares slope across a sliding window of recent steps. Furthermore, Ariadne applies an R² trend confidence discount: isolated exploration produces low fit quality and is discounted, whereas sustained, multi-step drift produces a tight linear fit that amplifies the risk score.
What happens if Ariadne crashes or encounters an unhandled exception? +
Ariadne operates with a strict FAIL_CLOSED posture by default. If an internal database disconnect or embedder error occurs, un-evaluated tool calls are rejected outright rather than silently forwarded to the tool backend. An internal fault can never be weaponized as an invisible bypass.
Can we test policy and threshold adjustments before deploying to production? +
Yes. Ariadne includes a built-in Policy Backtesting Engine and Minimum Intervention Finder. You can replay thousands of recorded production trajectories against a proposed threshold change to confirm zero false positives before saving the new configuration.
